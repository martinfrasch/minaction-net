## How the four tests fit together

Each subpage follows the same four-part structure:

- **The claim** — what NWAP predicts in this domain.
- **Method** — how the prediction was operationalised.
- **The result** — what was actually observed, with effect sizes.
- **What this domain adds to the programme** — the unique contribution of the test.

Read in order, the four pages tell a single story: NWAP is consistent with the modularity-excess signature in every domain where it has been tested, with the strongest quantitative case made by the biology paper (Tara Oceans, $\Delta Q \approx 0.40$ over the gold-standard bipartite null).
