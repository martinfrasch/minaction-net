## How the tests fit together

Each subpage follows the same four-part structure:

- **The claim** — what NWAP predicts in this domain.
- **Method** — how the prediction was operationalised.
- **The result** — what was actually observed, with effect sizes.
- **What this domain adds to the programme** — the unique contribution of the test.

Read in order, the first four pages tell a single story: NWAP is consistent with the modularity-excess (and energy-cost) signature in every observational or computational domain where it has been tested. The fifth page is different in kind, and its result is different: it is an intervention with a single control parameter, and the specific mechanism the framework's directed-graph extension assigned to the antisymmetric sector — structure preservation, circulation, a modularity signature — is refuted, while the weaker structural prediction holds. The programme treats that as its most informative result so far, because it is the first that could have failed and did. The **strongest engineering proof** comes from the **neural-architecture paper** (2,203 controlled experiments where the energy-regularised objective measurably outperforms prediction-error-only baselines), followed by the **physics paper** (where the Triple-Action functional recovers Kepler's and Hooke's laws at order-of-magnitude reduced training energy). The biology paper is the **natural-system population-scale signature** in field-collected metagenomic data ($\Delta Q \approx 0.40$ over the gold-standard bipartite null) — strong evidence that the predicted architectural pattern shows up in real ecosystems, but, like all observational data, consistent with rather than discriminating among neighbouring variational principles. The physiology paper ties the four together as the theoretical anchor.
