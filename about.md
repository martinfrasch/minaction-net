## About the programme

minAction.net is a single-investigator research programme led by **Martin&nbsp;G. Frasch, MD, PhD**, at the [Institute on Human Development and Disability](https://depts.washington.edu/uwihdd/), University of Washington School of Medicine, Seattle, WA, USA, and [Health Stream Analytics, LLC](https://healthstreamanalytics.com), Seattle, WA, USA.

The programme tests whether a single action functional — the **Network-Weighted Action** — can serve as a vertically organising principle across biological scales and, by analogy, across artificial learning systems. The four 2026 papers are the first integrated validation of this idea.
