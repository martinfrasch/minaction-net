## Living and learning systems organise toward a single functional.

The **Network-Weighted Action Principle (NWAP)** proposes that biological networks, neural architectures, and physical-law-discovery systems all extremise the same quantity — a balance of energy minimisation, information maximisation, and connection-cost penalty:

$$
S_{\text{NW}} \;=\; \int (E - I + A\!\cdot\!C)\,dt.
$$

The empirical prediction is sharp and falsifiable: networks under these constraints organise toward **high modularity**, with the modularity *excess* over appropriate null models scaling with optimisation pressure.

Over 2023–2026 we tested this prediction in four independent domains — **physiology**, **physics**, **neural architecture**, and **biology** — and the modularity-excess signature appears in each. This site is the integrated record of that programme.
